What's New

Every feature, improvement, and fix across all versions of TouchLock. See the user feedback behind them →

v1.5.4 Latest November 2026

A duress PIN for when you're forced to unlock, a cleaner toolbar popup, and update notices that tell you what's new.

Duress PIN PRO

Set a secret second PIN for when someone pressures you to unlock. It unlocks like your real PIN, but first erases what you choose: the history of specific sites, their history and sign-ins, or all browsing data. Set it up in Settings → Duress PIN. TouchLock only asks for the extra permissions it needs when you turn it on, and gives them back if you turn it off.

Redesigned Popup

The toolbar popup is slimmer and tidier. Settings sits in the header, Lock Now shows your shortcut, and the current site and auto-lock share one compact panel, with the site's icon.

Simpler Settings Page

Lock Behavior now shows just the basics on the Settings page, with fullscreen, video options and your shortcut on their own page. Lock Specific Sites and Duress PIN also open their own pages, so Settings stays short and easy to scan.

One Line for Links

Support, Bug, What's New and Developer now hide behind a ⋯ button next to the featured app, so the popup takes less space.

Update Notices Say What's New

When a new version is out, the popup, lock screen and Settings show a one-line summary of what it brings. Notices match the version your browser's store actually offers, and TouchLock checks once a day instead of only at startup.

Fullscreen Lock Plays Nice With Other Apps

With fullscreen lock on, a lock used to pull the browser in front of whatever you were doing and force it back to fullscreen when you switched away. Now the browser goes fullscreen only when you switch to it, and stays out of the way while you use other apps. The option is no longer marked "Recommended", since the lock keeps every page hidden either way.

Missing Icon Fixed

The featured app in the popup sometimes showed an empty icon. It now always shows its logo or icon.

v1.5.3 November 2026

A tidier Settings page, a fresh welcome page, and a fix for the toolbar badge that wouldn't go away.

"PIN" Badge Clears After Pinning

The "PIN" reminder on the toolbar icon sometimes stayed after you pinned TouchLock. It now disappears as soon as you pin it, and anyone still seeing it can clear it by opening the popup once.

Tidier Settings

Settings now opens in grid view (you can still switch to list). Security Questions is collapsed until you need it, while still showing whether it's set up, and the sidebar opens it for you.

Fullscreen Lock Warning

The fullscreen lock option now explains that, on some computers, fullscreen can make the browser freeze or stop responding, and that turning it off fixes it.

Easier Bug Reports

The Bug button in the popup now opens the feedback page. Uninstall feedback includes your TouchLock version, browser, operating system and which features you had set up, so problems can be fixed faster. No browsing data, PIN or answers are ever sent.

New Welcome Page

A redesigned welcome page shows every feature, a quicker 3-step setup, and your lock shortcut.

v1.5.2 November 2026

Fix: the idle lock no longer interrupts you while you're watching a video.

Idle Lock Waits While Videos Play

Watching YouTube, Netflix or any other video no longer triggers the idle lock, even though you aren't touching the keyboard or mouse. It's on by default and also covers videos embedded on other pages and picture-in-picture. Once the video stops, the browser locks after your usual idle time. Locking your computer still locks the browser right away.

Video Options

In Settings → Lock Behavior → Video options, choose any website or only the sites you pick (like youtube.com), count music and podcasts too, and set a safety limit (1, 2 or 4 hours) so the browser still locks if you walk away while something plays.

Pause Auto-Lock for an Hour

When idle lock is on, the toolbar popup has a Pause 1 hour button, with Resume to turn it back on early.

v1.5.1 November 2026

Security fix: nothing can be opened while the browser is locked, even after pressing Esc.

Stricter Lock

While locked, every page — websites, the lock tab's own address bar, and browser pages like Settings or Extensions — is sent to the lock screen before it opens. After you unlock, each tab returns to the page it was opening, so nothing is lost.

Esc No Longer Exits the Lock

With fullscreen lock on, pressing Esc used to leave fullscreen and show your tabs. TouchLock now returns the window to fullscreen right away while it's locked (Chrome, Edge and other Chromium browsers).

Stays Locked After a Restart

If the browser was closed while locked, it now opens locked again, even when "Lock on browser startup" is off.

v1.5.0 November 2026

Require your PIN every few hours, even when you use your fingerprint.

Require PIN Every… PRO

In Settings → Biometric Authentication, choose 1, 4, 6, 12 or 24 hours. After that time, Touch ID and Windows Hello stop working until you unlock once with your PIN — then your fingerprint works for another full period. Applies to the lock screen, locked sites, and settings confirmations. Off by default.

v1.4.1 November 2026

Patch release fixing fullscreen getting stuck and biometric registration in Firefox.

Browser No Longer Stuck in Fullscreen

After unlocking, Chrome could stay in fullscreen with the back, forward, and address bar hidden — especially after the browser was closed while locked. TouchLock now remembers which windows it made fullscreen and always brings them back out on unlock, while leaving your own fullscreen (F11) alone.

Fullscreen Lock Is Now Opt-In

The fullscreen lock screen is off by default and was turned off once for existing users. Turn it back on in Settings → Lock Behavior. If a window is still stuck in fullscreen, press F11 (Windows) or Ctrl+Cmd+F (Mac).

Biometrics in Firefox

Registering Touch ID or Windows Hello in Firefox failed with "The operation is insecure". It now works on Firefox 150 and later; your system prompt shows "touchlock.vercel.app", which is TouchLock. On older Firefox versions, Settings explains the requirement and the PIN keeps working.

Clearer Biometric Errors

If Firefox blocks registration because TouchLock's website access is turned off, Settings now says how to allow it instead of showing a technical error.

v1.4.0 November 2026

Lock specific sites like banking or email, even while the browser is unlocked.

Lock Specific Sites PRO

Add sites in Settings → Lock Specific Sites, or click "Lock this site" in the toolbar popup. A locked site asks for your PIN or fingerprint before it opens — the page never loads until you unlock it. Works in Chrome, Edge, and Firefox.

Choose When Sites Lock Again

Re-lock an unlocked site when you leave it or close all its tabs (default), after 5–60 minutes, or only when the browser locks. Sites always lock again when the browser locks or restarts.

Subdomain & Browser-Unlock Options

"Include subdomains" decides whether locking google.com also locks mail.google.com (on by default). "Keep sites locked after unlocking the browser" decides whether your sites still ask for your PIN or fingerprint after a browser unlock (on by default).

Settings Changes Need Your PIN

Changing your PIN, biometrics, or security questions, removing a locked site, changing locked-site settings, or resetting all data now asks for your PIN or fingerprint first.

Cleaner Popup

The lock status now sits in the header, and on websites Settings becomes a gear icon next to Lock Now to make room for the current site. The popup is also slightly larger and easier to read.

Follow Your Feedback

A new feedback tracker shows every reported bug and feature request with its progress. When you send feedback from Settings or after uninstalling, you can leave your email and we'll tell you when it's done — every report is handled within 7 days at most.

Firefox 115 or Later

TouchLock for Firefox now needs Firefox 115 or newer, which is required for keeping site-unlock state only until the browser closes.

v1.3.6 November 2026

Patch release fixing the missing TouchLock logo.

Logo Now Displays Correctly

The TouchLock logo was missing in the top-left corner of Settings, as well as in the popup, Welcome, and What's New pages, in store builds for Chrome, Edge, and Firefox. It now shows up everywhere.

v1.3.5 October 2026

Guided tour, fullscreen lock mode, auto-save settings, and Pro Features removed.

Interactive Guided Tour

A step-by-step tour auto-starts on first install, walking you through every section — PIN, Biometrics, Recovery, Lock Behavior, Feedback, and Sidebar — with detailed explanations and progress dots. Replay anytime via the compass icon in the sidebar.

Fullscreen Lock Screen

New option (enabled by default) that puts the browser into fullscreen when locked, hiding the address bar, tabs, and all browser UI to prevent bypassing the lock screen. Window state is saved and restored on unlock.

Auto-Save Lock Settings

Lock behavior toggles (startup lock, fullscreen, idle lock, idle timeout) now save instantly on every change. No more clicking a "Save" button — just toggle and go.

Build Script Branch Selector

The store package builder now lets you pick which branch to build from, with auto-stash and branch restore after the build completes.

Support Strip

Centered Content Layout

Responsive Sidebar

v1.2.0 September 2026

Flexible PIN length, keyboard shortcuts, and quality-of-life improvements.

Flexible PIN Length (4–10 digits)

Choose anywhere from 4 to 10 digits for your PIN. A live strength indicator shows Weak, Fair, Good, or Strong as you type.

Keyboard Shortcut to Lock

Instantly lock the browser with Alt+L (or Ctrl+L on Mac). Fully customizable from Settings — reserved browser shortcuts are blocked with clear warnings.

Firefox Auto-Shortcut Sync

On Firefox, custom shortcuts are automatically applied at the browser level via browser.commands.update() — no manual step needed.

What's New Notifications

After updates, a subtle notification dot in Settings lets you discover new features without any interruption.

v1.1.0 August 2026

First public release with full browser locking, biometrics, and recovery.

Biometric Unlock

Unlock with Touch ID on Mac or Windows Hello on PC. Auto-triggers when the lock screen loads — no clicks needed.

Profile-Level Lock

Blocks access to the entire browser profile — history, downloads, bookmarks, passwords — until you authenticate.

6-Digit PIN with SHA-256

Secure fallback PIN hashed with SHA-256 and random salt. Never stored in plaintext.

Tab Guards

Five layers of protection prevent switching tabs, opening new tabs, or closing the lock tab while locked.

Auto-Lock on Startup & Idle

Locks automatically on browser launch. Optional idle lock after configurable inactivity (1 min – 1 hour).

PIN Recovery via Security Questions

Set 3 security questions during setup. Answer any 2 correctly to reset a forgotten PIN.

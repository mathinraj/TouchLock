Lock your bank. Leave the rest open.

Some sites deserve a second door. A locked site asks for your fingerprint or PIN before it opens, even while the rest of the browser is unlocked.

Add one in Settings, or open the site and click Lock this site in the toolbar popup.

Step away and it locks. Watch a film and it waits.

Idle lock counts from 1 minute to 1 hour. While a video plays, the count holds, on any site or only the ones you choose.

The other PIN

New in 1.6 · PRO

Forced to unlock? Give them the other PIN.

A duress PIN opens the browser like normal. Before it does, it quietly erases the history, cookies and sign-ins of the sites you chose, or all browsing data.

Nothing leaves your device.

Local only
Your PIN, settings and locked sites stay in your browser's storage.
Hashed, never plain text
The PIN and security answers are hashed with SHA-256 and a random salt.
Biometrics stay with the OS
Touch ID and Windows Hello run through WebAuthn. Your fingerprint never reaches TouchLock.
No accounts, analytics or ads
There is nothing to sign up for and nothing phoning home.
Plain JavaScript, open source
No third-party libraries. Read every line on GitHub.

One honest limit: someone using an already unlocked browser can still remove the extension from its extensions page.

Install manually in any Chromium browser
  1. Open the latest release, download the ZIP for your browser (for example touchlock-for-chrome-<version>.zip) and extract it.
  2. Go to your extensions page: chrome://extensions, edge://extensions or brave://extensions.
  3. Turn on Developer mode in the top-right corner.
  4. Click Load unpacked and choose the extracted folder.
  5. Settings opens on its own. Set your PIN and you're done.

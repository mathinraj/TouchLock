TouchLock v1.3.0

New features & improvements

Redesigned Settings Page

A completely new settings experience with sidebar navigation, setup progress bar, and a cleaner layout. Switch between list and grid views with the floating toggle.

Pin-to-Toolbar Prompt

On first install, a helpful banner guides you to pin TouchLock to your toolbar for quick lock/unlock access. Auto-detects when pinned and shows a confirmation.

Grid & List View Toggle

A floating toggle lets you switch settings between a compact grid layout and a focused list layout. Your preference is saved automatically.

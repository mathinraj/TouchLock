What's New

TouchLock v1.4.0

New features & improvements

Lock Specific Sites

Keep sites like banking or email locked even while the browser is unlocked. Add them in Settings → Lock Specific Sites, or click "Lock this site" in the toolbar popup.

You Choose When Sites Lock Again

An unlocked site locks again when you leave it or close its tabs, after a set time, or only when the browser locks. You also choose whether subdomains are included.

Settings Changes Need Your PIN

Changing your PIN, biometrics, recovery questions, or locked sites now asks for your PIN or fingerprint first, so nobody can switch your protection off.

Cleaner Popup

Lock status now sits in the header. On websites, Settings becomes a gear icon next to Lock Now, making room for the current site and its "Lock this site" button.

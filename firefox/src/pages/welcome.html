Welcome to TouchLock

Lock your whole browser, or just your bank and email, behind your fingerprint or a PIN.

  • Everything stays on your device
  • No account needed
  • No tracking or ads

What TouchLock does

Fingerprint unlock

Unlock in a touch with Touch ID or Windows Hello. A PIN is always there as a backup, and can be asked for every few hours.

Lock specific sites PRO

Keep your bank or email locked, even while the rest of the browser is open.

Smart auto-lock

Locks on startup or when you step away, but waits while you're watching a video.

Nothing gets past it

Every tab, window and browser page stays behind the lock screen until you unlock.

Duress PIN PRO NEW

Forced to unlock? A secret second PIN opens the browser as normal, but first erases the sites you chose.

Easy recovery

Forgot your PIN? Answer 2 of your 3 security questions to set a new one.

Ready in 3 steps

  1. 1

    Create a PIN

    4–10 digits. It's your backup way to unlock.

  2. 2

    Add your fingerprint

    Register Touch ID or Windows Hello for one-touch unlocking.

  3. 3

    Choose when it locks

    On startup, after you've been away, or only the sites you pick.

Tip: press Alt + L on any page to lock your browser instantly.

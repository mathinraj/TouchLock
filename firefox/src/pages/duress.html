Duress PIN PRO

A secret second PIN for when someone forces you to unlock your browser. It unlocks like your real PIN, but first erases the data you choose below.

Off
Set up your PIN in Settings first. The duress PIN works alongside it.

How it works

  1. You choose a second PIN that's different from your real one.
  2. If you're ever pressured to unlock, type the duress PIN instead.
  3. TouchLock erases what you picked, then unlocks as normal. Nothing on screen shows that anything happened.

Works on the browser lock screen and on locked sites. Fingerprint unlock and settings changes always need your real PIN.

    Before you turn it on

    • Erased data can't be recovered. Not by you, and not by TouchLock.
    • Typing the duress PIN by mistake erases it too. Pick a PIN you won't enter by accident.
    • Your browser will ask to let TouchLock read and change your browsing history and clear browsing data. It's only used when the duress PIN is entered, and is removed if you turn this off.
